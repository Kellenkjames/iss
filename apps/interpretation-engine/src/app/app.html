How this demo works

A focused interpretation workflow

  1. Select a source record. Inspect the structured context that needs explanation.
  2. Frame the issue. Add the subject, evidence, and question to guide the interpretation.
  3. Review the result. Evaluate the concise answer without allowing it to replace human judgment.
  4. Keep the AI in support mode. The engine demonstrates reasoning, not autonomous action.
