How this demo works

A guided review flow

  1. Select a signal. Choose the operational issue you want to review.
  2. Inspect the evidence. Use the subject, evidence, and question fields to frame the review.
  3. Request interpretation. Let the AI provide a concise operational summary.
  4. Keep human judgment in control. Accept, defer, or escalate as the final decision.
