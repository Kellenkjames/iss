# ISS Product Documentation Instructions

Treat documents under `docs/product/` as approved product scope and requirement sources.

## Required behavior

- Preserve the intent and boundaries of each PRD.
- Do not invent features, personas, workflows, acceptance criteria, or dependencies.
- Do not broaden an MVP requirement into a platform-level capability without approval.
- Identify missing or ambiguous requirements explicitly.
- Distinguish product requirements from engineering implementation decisions.
- Distinguish product requirements from visual or interaction design decisions.
- Keep filenames lowercase and hyphenated.
- Preserve requirement traceability when revising a document.

## Decision authority

Human leadership owns final approval for:

- product vision,
- scope,
- requirements,
- prioritization,
- acceptance criteria.

GPT may assist with analysis and clarification, but inferred requirements are not approved requirements.

## Validation

Before completing a product-document change:

- state which requirement or scope boundary changed,
- identify affected PRDs or downstream design/engineering documents,
- confirm whether human approval is required,
- avoid modifying unrelated product scope.
